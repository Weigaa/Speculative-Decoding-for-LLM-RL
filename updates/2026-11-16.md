## Newly included studies

- [REAL Loss](../papers/tian2026real.md) — serving transfer. Uses accepted-path importance weights and conditional marginalization to optimize expected speculative acceptance length rather than a product of marginal acceptance surrogates.
- [Olifant speculative drafting](../papers/vandenbosch2026olifant.md) — serving transfer. Uses an offline-trained information-gain-ordered memory trie to propose next tokens from four-token contexts on CPU, with cached-verifier rejection fast paths and separate latency/energy budget analysis.
- [BunshinRL](../papers/huang2026bunshinrl.md) — contextual. Forks live agent workspaces with copy-on-write sharing, caches repeated tool prefixes, and executes predicted historical tool calls during model reasoning with signature checks and rollback.

REAL is a fixed-target drafter-loss study. It reports probabilistic serving acceptance and, separately, batch-80 output throughput. Neither metric establishes target-policy RL training acceleration. Olifant is a CPU memory-based classifier with greedy verification on consumer hardware; stochastic rollout and batched server-GPU transfer remain untested. Its INLG acceptance is confirmed on the official list, while the reviewed full text is the arXiv version. BunshinRL directly evaluates agentic GRPO, but speculation targets tool execution and workspace state, so it is contextual rather than a new token-level speculative-decoding core method.
