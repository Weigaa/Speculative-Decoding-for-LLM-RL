## Newly curated records

- [ForeSpark](../papers/song2026forespark.md) — serving transfer. Predicts acceptance before drafting and jointly allocates per-request draft lengths and a batch-wide verification budget.
- [DForkTree](../papers/zheng2026dforktree.md) — serving transfer. Uses target-generated n-gram statistics to correct branch scores after candidate-tree forks and constructs the selected tree on the GPU.
- [D3Tree](../papers/nie2026d3tree.md) — serving transfer. Learns conditional node survival and prunes ancestor-closed draft trees under a load-dependent batch-wide verification budget.
- [ChainFlow](../papers/aktas2026chainflow.md) — serving transfer. Predicts target-conditioned latent states in one or two flow steps, then uses token-feedback heads and tree emission for candidate generation.
- [D3D](../papers/chen2026d3d.md) — serving transfer. Uses a standalone diffusion drafter to prepare successor blocks for possible verification outcomes concurrently with target verification.
- [TraceSpec](../papers/zhao2026tracespec.md) — serving transfer. Combines agent trajectory replay and dynamic execution with state-conditioned allocation of a fixed drafter-supervision budget.
- [Phrase-level drafting](../papers/naidu2026phrase.md) — serving transfer. Predicts entries of a separate phrase vocabulary from frozen target features, expands each phrase into target tokens, and verifies candidate paths.
- [Processor-contract diagnostics](../papers/ding2026processorcontracts.md) — contextual. Checks whether logits processors see the correct verified prefix and rolled-back state at every speculative position.
- [Adversarial speculative reasoning](../papers/luo2026adversarial.md) — contextual. Measures verification-window numerical effects in long-horizon agents and trains a drafter to induce rejection near selected reasoning decisions.
- [SPEC-Distill](../papers/liu2026specdistill.md) — direct rl. Uses offline teacher trajectories as early RL speculative prefixes, then switches to refreshed student-rollout history without online teacher scoring.

## Evidence and version decisions

SPEC-Distill is a formal October EMNLP 2026 publication recovered by supplementary search. It directly evaluates GRPO training, but its published Section 3.3 omits residual correction and calls the rollout semi-on-policy. It is distinct from SPEC-RL, and is not reclassified as lossless sampling.

D3D uses a second GPU for asynchronous drafting. Its headline comparison with single-GPU synchronous baselines does not establish a resource-normalized advantage. D3Tree and phrase-level drafting are greedy inference studies. TraceSpec separates fixed-prefix replay improvements from dynamic episode timing. BADC processor diagnostics and adversarial speculative reasoning are contextual and excluded from the core count.

Acceptance-aware training v2, TailSieve v3 and Uni-LaSpec v2 were reviewed. Their original canonical versions and previous edition measurements remain available. TailSieve headline values retain their original uniform-routing baseline, with matched-decoder routing controls recorded separately.
